Aerial Services

Aerial data for large projects

Drone-based aerial photography, mapping and modelling for infrastructure, engineering, construction and real estate projects. One team, from flight plan to final deliverable.

Contact Us

Service lines

Aerial photography, mapping and modelling

We capture large sites, construction complexes, quarries and infrastructure routes from the air, and fly them again on a set schedule so you can follow progress over time.

Every flight becomes data your teams can work with: orthophotos, 3D models and terrain models for planning, surveying, volume calculations, quality control and engineering documentation.

Orthophoto maps · 3D models · terrain models

Flight, operation and integration

Developing an unmanned system? Our licensed, experienced pilots fly and operate drones and UAVs of every size and configuration on your behalf.

We support field trials and development programmes, help integrate new systems, and run operations for clients worldwide.

From flight plan to final output

  1. Plan the mission

    We match the aircraft, sensors and flight plan to the size of your project and what you need from it.

  2. Collect in the field

    Our crews fly the site and collect the data, once or on a regular schedule.

  3. Process the data

    We turn the raw imagery into measurable maps and models.

  4. Deliver outputs

    Orthophotos, 3D models and terrain models, ready for your engineers and planners.
