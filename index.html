APEX LT · UAV fleet management and ground operations platform

One Platform. Complete Fleet Visibility.

See the bigger picture. Plan with confidence. Stay connected to every part of your operation.

APEX LT brings fleet overview, mission planning and technician diagnostics together in one ground-side platform, for every Phoenix customer.

Contact Us

Three integrated capabilities

Fleet management and control

One view. Your entire fleet.

  • Multi-aircraft fleet overview in a single interface
  • Map-based aircraft visualisation and fleet awareness
  • Centralised status and event visibility
  • A scalable interface for growing fleets

Mission planning

Plan smarter. Prepare with confidence.

  • Structured mission-planning workspace
  • Route and waypoint visualisation
  • Mission information organised in one place
  • Plan review and validation workflow

Know more. Maintain better.

To make effective long-range deterrence accessible to the nations that need it
