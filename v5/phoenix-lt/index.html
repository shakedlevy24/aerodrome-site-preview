Airframe sections

Components

  1. CameraStabilised nose camera with real-time video over the data link.
  2. Communication antennasDual-redundant, encrypted links for command, telemetry and video.
  3. GNSS antennasTwo multi-constellation receivers (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou) with RTK.
  4. Warhead sectionUp to 50 kg, carried in the section behind the nose.
  5. Fuel tankIntegral composite tank with baffles that keep the fuel steady in manoeuvres.
  6. EngineFour-cylinder, air-cooled two-stroke boxer engine with a carbon pusher propeller.
  7. Airspeed sensorsPitot tubes on both wings measure airspeed for the autopilot.
  8. ElevonsTwo control surfaces on each wing, each with its own servo, for redundancy.
