Who we are

This website is operated by [registered company name and company number] ("Aerodrome", "we", "us"). Aerodrome is the controller of the personal data collected through this website.

Privacy contact: privacy@aerodrome-sys.com.

Data we collect

We collect only the data you choose to give us and a small amount of technical data needed to run the site.

  • Contact form: first and last name, work email, company, country, the type of enquiry you select, and the content of your message.
  • Job applications: the details you send us when you apply for a role, such as your CV, contact details and work history.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and the date and time of the visit, recorded in server logs.

You are not legally required to give us any personal data. Providing it is your choice and depends on your consent. If you do not provide the details marked as required in a form, we will not be able to answer that enquiry.

Please do not send us personal data about other people unless they know about it and agree.

Why we use it

We use personal data only for these purposes:

  • to answer your enquiry and stay in contact with you about it;
  • to assess whether and how we can work with you or your organisation, including the checks that export-control and sanctions laws require of us;
  • to consider job applications;
  • to keep the website secure and working, and to understand in aggregate how it is used;
  • to meet our legal obligations and to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for automated decisions about you, and we do not send marketing emails unless you have asked for them.

Who receives it

Inside Aerodrome, your data is seen only by the people who need it to handle your enquiry. Outside Aerodrome, we share it only with:

  • service providers that host this website and its forms, deliver our email and store our records, under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only for us;
  • professional advisers such as lawyers and auditors, where needed;
  • government authorities, regulators or courts, where the law requires it, including export-control authorities;
  • a buyer or successor, if all or part of our business is sold or reorganised.

Some of these recipients may be located outside Israel, including in countries whose data-protection laws differ from Israeli law. Where that is the case we take the steps the law requires to keep the data protected.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising cookies and does not track you across other websites.

The site may store a small amount of information in your browser that is strictly necessary for it to work. Its fonts are delivered by Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google when it loads them.

If we add analytics to measure visits, we will use it only to produce aggregate statistics, we will update this section to name the tool, and where the law requires your consent we will ask for it first.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above.

  • Enquiries: for as long as we are in contact, and up to [24 months] after the last contact.
  • Job applications: up to [12 months] after the hiring process ends, unless you agree to a longer period.
  • Server logs: up to [12 months].

We may keep data longer where the law requires it or where we need it to establish or defend legal claims. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

How we protect it

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data, including access controls and encryption in transit. No website or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, you have the right to review the personal data we hold about you and to ask us to correct or delete data that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or out of date.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have the right to ask for erasure, to restrict or object to processing, to receive your data in a portable format, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Where those laws apply, we rely on your consent, on steps taken at your request before a contract, on our legal obligations, and on our legitimate interest in running and securing our business.

To use any of these rights, write to privacy@aerodrome-sys.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will answer within the time the law sets.

Children

This website is intended for professional audiences. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Links to other sites

This website may link to websites we do not operate, such as LinkedIn. Their privacy practices are their own, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Material changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your personal data: privacy@aerodrome-sys.com.
